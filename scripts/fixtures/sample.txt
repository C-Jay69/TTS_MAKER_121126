The Whispering Valley

It was a bright cold day in April, and the clocks were striking thirteen. Winston walked quickly through the narrow streets, past the old bakery that still smelled of warm bread despite the early hour.

He carried a leather satchel over one shoulder. Inside it lay a folded map, a brass compass, and a letter he had read so many times that the creases had begun to tear.

The valley, the letter said, remembers every voice that passes through it. Travelers speak of echoes that answer questions no one asked aloud.
